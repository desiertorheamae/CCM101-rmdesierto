# Mission Reflection

This mission helped me understand the differences between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three platforms, Google Cloud impressed me the most because of its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I found these features useful because they are important technologies in modern cloud computing.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide services for virtual machines, storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. They also provide management consoles that allow users to create, manage, and monitor cloud resources. Although their services have different names, many of them perform similar functions.

Business requirements have an important role in choosing a cloud platform. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, location, security requirements, scalability, and type of workload. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while a company focused on AI and data may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not only about selecting the most popular platform. The recommendation should be based on what the client actually needs. Comparing the services and requirements helped me see how cloud solutions can be matched to different business situations.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added research about the three major cloud providers, comparison tables, business recommendations, and Linux investigation results. Organizing these activities in GitHub also helped me practice documenting my work in a clear and organized way. Overall, this mission improved my understanding of cloud computing and gave me more confidence in comparing and recommending cloud services.
